As I journeyed into the heart of the writer through the pages of Agony of Beauty, I was drawn into a story that explores the complexities of human life and emotion. The novel highlights the consequences of hasty decisions, such as Beauty's parents marrying without carefully considering the future, her mother entrusting her daughter to someone she barely knew, Beauty making the wrong decision out of fear, and Sister Tope choosing the wrong life partner.
